About Train Direct

Train Direct helps independent soccer trainers organize individual and group sessions. A trainer can choose players, a time, and a location, then confirm who actually attended. Payment tracking ties each player’s charge to the session so trainers can see what has been paid and what is still due.

The goal is to spend less time sorting out session details and more time coaching on the field.
